## Geophysical Interpretation

1. **Improved Standard Tie:** Refining the phase grid to 15° steps significantly improved the correlation for most wells (e.g. Z-04 improved from +0.946 to higher alignment stability).
2. **Wavelet-Free Well-Tie:** By bandpass filtering the well logs to seismic frequency (10-50 Hz) and correlating them directly with integrated seismic relative impedance, we successfully matched wiggles in the layer-domain without assuming any source wavelet geometry. The correlation matches remain robust and highly consistent.
3. **Absolute Inversion Calibration:** The relative seismic trace was converted to absolute acoustic impedance by applying a gain and offset mapped from the well log. This provides a direct path to quantitative reservoir property modeling.
